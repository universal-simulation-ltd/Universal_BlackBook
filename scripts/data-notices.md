# Data

## GeoNames — countries, divisions and towns (public/geo/)

<https://www.geonames.org>
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) — <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

The place lists BlackBook suggests from (City, County, Country), finds the
nearest town in, and places map pins with are built by
`scripts/geo-build.mjs` from GeoNames' `countryInfo.txt`,
`admin1CodesASCII.txt`, `admin2Codes.txt` and `cities5000.txt`, reduced to a
name, a rounded position, a country, a division and a population per town.
© GeoNames, used under CC BY 4.0. Changes: filtered and reformatted as above.
